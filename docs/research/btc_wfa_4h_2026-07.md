# BTC 4h walk-forward: BBRSI vs SuperTrend+EMA200 vs CDC ActionZone vs SMC+FVG

July 2026 run of `scripts/btc_wfa_multi_strategy.py` on BTCUSDT 4h
(data.binance.vision spot klines, 2020-01 → 2026-07-18), long + short for all
four strategies. Raw per-window results: `core/btc_wfa/wfa_results.jsonl`
(gitignored cache; re-run `fetch` + `run` to regenerate).

## Protocol

* **Walk-forward:** anchored rolling window — train **6 calendar months**,
  optimize the per-strategy grid on train only, replay the winning override on
  the following **1 month** out-of-sample, then roll forward 1 month.
  66 test months (2021-01 → 2026-06), 264 strategy-windows total, zero errors.
* **No look-ahead:**
  * signals are decided on the last CLOSED bar and fill at the NEXT bar open
    (repo `ReplayEngine` semantics);
  * parameters are chosen from train data only;
  * each test month gets a 300-bar warmup lead-in of PRE-month bars that only
    warms indicators — `run_plugin_replay(min_bars_override=...)` starts
    trading at the month's first bar, so OOS stats contain no warmup trades;
  * phase labels use only data available BEFORE the test month starts.
* **Costs:** production fee/slippage/funding resolution from `bot_config.yaml`,
  same config resolver as live/backtest parity paths.
* **Phases:** each test month is labeled from the 1d chart at the last daily
  close before the month starts — `bull` = close > EMA200 and EMA200 rising,
  `bear` = close < EMA200 and EMA200 falling, else `sideways`.
  Split: 38 bull / 23 bear / 5 sideways months.
* **Selection metric:** highest train net profit among combos with >= 3 train
  trades (fallback: all combos).

## Sizing caveat (read before comparing rows)

`xauby_actionzone` runs its live CDC-pure profile: no stop loss,
fixed-fraction `position_pct: 0.95` compounding — monthly swings are large by
construction. The other three strategies size risk-based
(`risk_pct` ≈ 3% of equity per SL distance), so their monthly percentages are
structurally much smaller. Compare **sign, consistency and drawdown**, not raw
magnitude, across that boundary.

## Parameter stability (top train-selected combos across 66 windows)

* **bbrsi_mean_reversion** — `bb_std=2.5, rsi_oversold=25, sl=1.8×ATR`
  (27/66) then `bb_std=2.5, rsi_oversold=30` (23/66). Avg OOS trade win rate
  61.4%, but losers outweigh winners: mean reversion on BTC 4h loses after
  costs in every phase.
* **supertrend_ema200** — selection oscillates between `mult=2.5` and
  `mult=3.5` (both with `exit_on_ema_loss=True` dominant). Win rate 42.6%
  with trend-following payoff skew; the only strategy positive in all three
  phases.
* **xauby_actionzone** — `fresh_zone_window=1, exit_on_bear_cross=False`
  dominates (47/66 across ap 1/2). Win rate 37.5%; stop-and-reverse without a
  stop bleeds hard in bear months (-47.7% compounded across the 23 bear
  months) at 0.95 fixed-fraction sizing.
* **xauby_smc_pro** — `confluence_min_score=1.0, require_liquidity_sweep=False`
  (37/66); requiring the sweep rarely wins train. Flat-to-slightly-negative
  overall; inactive in 15 of 66 months.

## Reading

1. **SuperTrend+EMA200 long+short is the only configuration that survives the
   whole walk-forward** (+12.4% compounded at 3% risk sizing, positive in
   bull, bear and sideways). Modest, but the sign is stable and the worst
   month is -3.0%.
2. **CDC ActionZone's XAU live profile does not transfer to BTC 4h.** The
   same stop-and-reverse, no-SL, 0.95-fraction profile that passes on
   PAXG/XAU whipsaws on BTC — especially in bear phases. Any BTC deployment
   would need its own sweep (different fresh-zone/exit settings, a stop, or
   much smaller sizing), not a copy of the gold config.
3. **BBRSI mean reversion is consistently negative in every phase** despite a
   61% trade win rate — the classic mean-reversion profile of many small wins
   erased by fewer larger losses plus fees. Not a BTC 4h candidate as-is.
4. **SMC+FVG is cost-neutral at best** — close to zero everywhere, slightly
   negative in bear months. The structure gates trade rarely enough that fees
   and slippage absorb the edge.
5. Bear-month shorts kept SuperTrend near flat while buy-and-hold BTC was deep
   red; CDC's bear losses come from being whipsawed in BOTH directions, not
   from shorting per se. The mean-reversion short side did not rescue BBRSI.

## Repro

```bash
python scripts/btc_wfa_multi_strategy.py fetch
python scripts/btc_wfa_multi_strategy.py run --jobs 4      # ~70 min on 4 cores
python scripts/btc_wfa_multi_strategy.py report --md docs/research/btc_wfa_4h_2026-07.md
```

Short support for `bbrsi_mean_reversion` and `supertrend_ema200` is gated by
`enable_short` (default `false`) added in this change; live long-only behavior
is unchanged. `xauby_actionzone` and `xauby_smc_pro` already shipped shorts.
